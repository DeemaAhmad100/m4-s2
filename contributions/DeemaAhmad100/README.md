# Revenue by City - Amman Digital Market
Finding:
Amman significantly dominates revenue generation in the Amman Digital Market, contributing approximately 32% of total revenue — more than double the second highest city.
This heavy concentration in the capital presents both an opportunity and a risk. While it drives strong performance, the business remains vulnerable to local market changes in Amman. Expanding into secondary cities is recommended for more balanced and sustainable growth.
